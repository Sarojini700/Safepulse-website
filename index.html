Smart SOS Safety Bracelet

Style meets safety.

Help is just three taps away. SafePulse is an elegant, discreet bracelet with a compact electronics section designed to trigger an SOS alert, obtain GPS location and send a normal SMS to trusted emergency contacts through the cellular network.

Problem / solution

When seconds matter, fewer steps can matter too.

01

The problem

In an emergency, a person may not have enough time to unlock a phone, find an app, navigate a screen and call for help.

02

The SafePulse solution

A quick physical triple-press trigger is built directly into a discreet bracelet, followed by GPS location and a cellular SMS alert to trusted contacts.
